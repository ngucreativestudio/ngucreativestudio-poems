We stand at the crossing where the old light bends,
Hands cupped to the hush between words and wind,
Promising not to forget what memory lends:
The weight of names, the maps our hearts have pinned.

By fire and frost, by the root and the ridge,
By the quiet that keeps the dawn from falling,
We bind our breath with the land as our bridge,
And listen for the bell of the future calling.

Let this be ledger and lantern and law,
An oath sealed in the margin of morning.
May the small and the brave always recall
How hope is the seed, and love the adjoining.

We pledge in ink that no silence may sever
The threads that stitch one soul to another;
A covenant kept in the marrow forever,
A promise made to sister and brother.

So move with the moon when she rises to guide,
Carry the pact through the storm and the calm;
With open palms and the horizon wide,
We guard what is gentle, we keep what is balm.

Here is our pact: to remember, to tend,
To speak when it's needed and heal when we mend.
